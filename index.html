Presslane is the storefront and back office for print and embroidery shops. Customers see their logo on the product before they order. Every quote is chased, every stage is messaged, the phone is answered and the floor updates the system by scanning a sheet. The chasing, answering and tracking are done by AI in your shop's name, so the only thing left to do is make the job.

Independent shops of 1 to 10 people, garment decorators, trade printers, sign makers and multi-site groups. UK first.

The floor updates the system.

Every order prints a runsheet with a barcode the moment it is confirmed. Scan it at each bench and the status changes and the customer is messaged. No PC, no typing, no walking to the office. Mono laser, any barcode scanner, and a pen if the scanner is down.

Each scan moves the job one stage.
