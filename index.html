A print shop loses money in the gaps between jobs, not in the jobs.

The quote that was never chased. The customer who rang three times to ask where their hoodies were. The job that sat finished on a bench because nobody updated the system. Every one of these is time the owner spent not printing.

Quotes

Most quotes that do not convert were never followed up. Shops are too busy printing to chase.

Calls

“Where is my order?” is the most common inbound call. It has an answer; nobody has time to give it.

Status

Our belief

The shop should run itself between the moments a person needs to be involved. Presslane exists to make those moments the only ones a printer has to touch.

Customers stop ringing to ask. When they do ring, the phone knows.

The phone is answered in your shop's name by an assistant that knows every order, every proof and every stock line. It reads numbers in groups of two, offers a text when the job ships, and hands to a person the moment someone asks.

  • Answers stock and status. Order number or surname is enough to find the job.
  • Logs the call to the order. Notes and follow-ups land on the job, not in a voicemail.
  • Never denies what it is. It does not say it is automated unless asked, and never pretends otherwise.
