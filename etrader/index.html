Moving over

How to leave eTrader without losing your website

Leaving eTrader, step by step

  1. Check who holds your domain. If it is in eTrader’s name, ask in writing to put it in yours.
  2. Keep your email where it is. We move mailboxes before the domain, so nothing is lost.
  3. We build the new shop on a Presslane address while your eTrader site stays live.
  4. Move the domain on a day you choose. One setting for a .co.uk, a transfer code for a .com. A few hours.
  5. Then give notice. Only once the new shop answers on your own domain.

We do the technical steps with you on a call, and check eTrader’s current terms before the move.
