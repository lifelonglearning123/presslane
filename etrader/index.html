How to leave eTrader without losing your website

How to leave eTrader without losing your website

Your domain name is yours. eTrader’s terms say it is returned to you when you leave. Here is the order to do things in, so nothing goes dark.

  1. Check who holds the domain. Look it up on a WHOIS site. If it is in your name, you are fine. If it is in eTrader’s name, ask them in writing to put it in yours. Do this first; it can take a few days.
  2. Keep your email where it is for now. If eTrader also runs your email, we move your mailboxes before the domain, so no message is lost.
  3. Let us build the new shop. It runs on a Presslane address while you check it. Your eTrader site stays live.
  4. Move the domain on a day you choose. For a .co.uk, eTrader changes one setting (the IPS tag) to point the domain at us. For a .com, they take the transfer lock off and give you a transfer code. Either takes a few hours to a day.
  5. Then cancel eTrader. Give notice only once the new shop answers on your own domain.

We do the technical steps with you on a call. If anything in eTrader’s terms has changed, we check it with them before the move.
